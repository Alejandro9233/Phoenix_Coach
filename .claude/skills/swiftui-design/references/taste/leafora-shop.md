# Leafora (plant shop concept) — leafora-shop.jpg

**Alex:** the cards and the glass look. Frosted panels over a deep green
photo, hairline edge, soft inner glow, big radius, image sitting inside the
card. That is the feel he wants the cards to have.

**Observed, for porting (Claude's read, not Alex's words):** one hue family
(dark green) with a single soft accent, never a second color. Photography
carries the mood; the chrome stays quiet and the glass reads as material
because there is a blurred image behind it. Category chips are glass too, the
selected one filled. Phoenix is near-black with white as the accent and has
no photography, so the steal is the card material and depth, not the palette
or the imagery: more blur, a visible hairline, a faint top-edge highlight,
and one level of inner panel. It confirms `.glassCard()` is the right idea
and says it should feel thicker than it does now.
