# Common thread across the tone references (Claude's synthesis, 2026-09-12)

Alex sent four tone images (runner-jan28, data-mountain, solvana-glow,
exhausted-privilege) and one UI image (leafora-shop). Read this first.

- **Black and white, full stop.** Every tone reference is monochrome. Color
  only where it is data (zones, status). Matches the app's rule; raises the
  bar on discipline.
- **Grain, blur, and glow are the material.** Not gradients, not shadows.
  Texture makes the dark feel deep. One glowing white subject per screen at
  most.
- **Data as texture, not chart junk.** Dust and density over clean strokes;
  tiny mono numbers as decoration around one big figure.
- **Sparse, editorial, mono-flavored text.** Five words that mean something
  about effort and process. Micro-labels pinned to corners. Light-weight
  hero copy, bold tracked micro-chrome.
- **Quiet CTAs.** Small outlined or soft-filled pills. Never loud.
- **From Leafora, only the cards:** thick frosted glass, hairline edge,
  faint top highlight, one inner panel level.

What this means for `/variants`: at least one of the five should push the
grain/glow/density direction hard, and none should add color, gradients, or
imagery that isn't data.
